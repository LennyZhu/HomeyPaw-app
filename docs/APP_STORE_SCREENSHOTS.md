# App Store Screenshot Plan

Target the Hong Kong Traditional Chinese storefront first, then capture the same states in English. Do not generate final marketing screenshots from fixtures that expose emails or invite codes.

1. **一家人共同記錄毛孩** — Home with pet hero and recent family activity.
2. **相片成長時間線** — Journal with polished multi-photo entries and date hierarchy.
3. **今日照顧一目了然** — Home “今日照顧” with Owner and Member records.
4. **家庭提醒不遺漏** — Reminders with today and future tasks.
5. **重溫那年今日** — Memory card with a meaningful older photo.
6. **只與信任的家人共享** — Family member screen without showing a live invite code.

Capture on a modern 6.7-inch iPhone size plus the additional App Store sizes required at submission time. Use genuine in-app UI, hide status-bar personal data, keep copy inside safe margins, and avoid implying Chat, remote push, medical advice, AI, or public social features.
